1. Overview

AI & ML Course: Learn Python is an offline-first learning app. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you personally.

Your learning data — lesson progress, quiz scores, streaks and settings — is stored only on your device. We have no server, and that data is never transmitted to us.

This policy explains the limited data that is handled by third-party services we use: Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases).

2. Information stored on your device

The following is created and stored locally on your device, and never leaves it unless you back up your device yourself:

  • Lesson completion status and reading position
  • Quiz scores and attempt counts
  • Daily streak and activity dates
  • App settings — theme and text size
  • Your premium unlock status

We cannot read this data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

3. Information collected by third-party services

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of this app displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • Device information — model, operating system version, language
  • Ad interaction data — impressions and clicks

We do not receive, store, or have access to this data. It is handled by Google under its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you purchase the premium unlock, the advertising SDK is not initialised at all and no advertising data is collected by this app.

Controlling ad personalisation. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app will show a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ads are served, and you may withdraw or change that consent at any time from the app's Settings screen.

3.2 Purchases — Google Play Billing

The premium unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles the transaction and provides the app with a purchase confirmation token so it can unlock content.

We never see or receive your payment card details, billing address, or Google account information. The purchase token is stored locally on your device only, to keep your unlock active offline.

Google's handling of your purchase is governed by:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Practice exercises

Practice exercises run entirely on your device as offline multiple-choice questions. No code you interact with is uploaded, and nothing about your answers leaves the app.

A future version may offer an optional download of a Python engine so exercises can execute real code. That download does not exist in this version. If it is added, this policy will be updated before that build is published, and the download will only ever happen if you explicitly tap to accept it.

7. Data retention

Data stored on your device is retained until you delete it in-app or uninstall the app. We hold no data of yours on any server, so we have no retention period to apply. Data held by Google in connection with advertising and purchases is retained per Google's own policies, linked above.
